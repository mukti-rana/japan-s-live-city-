# `/` (the homepage) — overrides to MASTER.md

Only the deltas. Everything not listed here follows MASTER.md. Approved by the project owner
(2026-11-16): the homepage is a premium, pitch-black, cinematic, vertically scrolling experience.
These overrides apply to the homepage only; MASTER.md is unchanged.

| Rule in MASTER | Override here | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Page surface `--surface-0` is `#0a0a0b` | The page and its shell are **`#000000`** (cards `#0b0b0c`, raised `#141416`) | Cinematic photography needs true black around it. |
| Both themes follow the toggle | The homepage stays **pitch-black in both themes**. Top bar, mobile drawer and footer take the same dark palette while `/` is shown (`[data-home-root]` + `:has`, no flash). Every other page follows the toggle. | A black cinematic page is the point; the toggle still works everywhere else. |
| ≤ 1–2 animated elements; no entrance choreography on dense views | **Chapter reveals** (updated 2026-11-17, approved; replay and per-section styles added the same day): each block animates every time it scrolls into view and is re-armed once completely off screen (so it plays again in both scroll directions, and returns from above when scrolling up). Styles: hero `scale` (slow fade, settle from 98.5%), `up` (Your Day, weather), `image` (Explore photographs, staggered), `slide-left`/`slide-right` (News, 72px, tablet and up), `left`/`right` (40px), `stagger` (Community photo cards, QuickAccess tiles), `pop` (numbers, buttons), `line` (hairlines). Earlier text, still true: each block fades in as it scrolls into view, `opacity`/`transform` only, ~700ms. On a phone every block rises 24px; from 768px, side-by-side blocks slide in from their own side by at most 40px, headings from the left and links from the right, the chapter hairline draws itself, and siblings stagger by ≤ 0.12s (≤ 0.4s of delay in any chapter heading). Plain CSS plus one IntersectionObserver per block. **Hero photographs**: a slow crossfade (8.5s hold, 1.6s fade) through the detected city's own photographs; it holds while the tab is hidden or the hero is off screen, has a visible pause button, and is a single still photograph under reduced motion. **One** slow hero zoom. Light parallax (≤ 6% travel) on place photographs only — never on text. At most **one** pinned section (none planned). No scroll-jacking. Under reduced motion everything renders in its final state. Animation pauses when the tab is hidden. | Scroll storytelling pattern; the skill's rules for parallax and pinning are followed. |

Body, UI and Japanese body text stay Space Grotesk / Noto Sans JP. | The homepage is the front door (same reasoning as `login.md`). |

Layout rules for this page:

- **Sidebar (updated 2026-11-17, approved):** one slim sidebar on every page. At ≥ 1024px it is a 56px black icon rail that opens to 240px on hover or keyboard focus as an overlay (it never pushes the page); off the home page a visitor can pin it open (remembered), which makes room for it. On `/` it floats, translucent, over the left edge of the photograph (the page keeps a 76px minimum side gutter so no text sits under it). No colour blocks, no permanent red Emergency block; the current page is a quiet highlight with `aria-current`. Below 1024px the phone menu button and drawer are unchanged. The earlier "hidden on `/`" rule is superseded.
- The **dock is home-only and desktop-only**, with **Community first**, a divider, then the existing eight tools.
- Chapters keep their reading order in the DOM with all motion off. Real data only; every live surface keeps its DEMO / Live / stale / empty / error state.
